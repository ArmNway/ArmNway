Effortless input

Unified income & expenses

Most stalls still write sales in a paper notebook. Recording here takes the same 30 seconds, and it turns into a live profit picture.

  • 4.1
    IncomeGrab orders sync on their own. Bank QR sales come from a screenshot, cash is spoken in the merchant's dialect, and Grab Tap to Pay (coming 2027) fills in the rest.
  • 4.2
    ExpenseSnap a market bill and MeeTang reads every line. MeeTang also reads the menu (Som Tam, Larb, Gai Yang, Tam Talay…) and suggests the usual costs: pork, chicken, seafood, papaya, herbs, pla ra. The merchant taps what they bought and adjusts the list.

Try: tap the mic, Upload, Snap receipt, a suggested cost, Why? or Adjust, or any cost line to edit it.
